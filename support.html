2. What BillShield Does (Current Design)

BillShield helps you organize and learn from billing paperwork. Core behavior:

  • Local-first storage: Scanned bills and EOBs are stored in on-device SQLite by default.
  • On-device analysis: OCR (when available on your device), rule-based charge review, denial-code explanations, estimated appeal timelines, and appeal letter drafts work without Cloud AI.
  • Active Cases: Save open billing disputes to track linked documents, status, and deadlines (limits depend on your plan).
  • Optional Cloud AI: Off by default. When you sign in and enable it, selected document text may be sent through our U.S.-hosted backend (ai-proxy on Supabase) to OpenAI for deeper analysis. Each scan requires a separate per-document opt-in.
  • Reference pricing: Price Compare and Medicare-style badges use bundled illustrative data — not live hospital price files or your insurer’s negotiated rates.
  • Resources directory: Curated links and phone numbers for financial assistance and advocacy — verify details with each organization before relying on them.
  • No automatic filing: We do not submit appeals, negotiate with providers, or guarantee savings or coverage decisions.

2.1 Quick start

  1. Scan or import a bill or EOB (camera or PDF).
  2. Review flagged lines, denial codes, and educational savings estimates.
  3. Save an Active Case if you want to track a dispute over time.
  4. Check estimated deadlines (internal appeal, external review, state complaint windows) — always confirm with your plan documents.
  5. Edit and export appeal letter PDFs when ready; you submit them yourself.
  6. Optional: Sign in with Apple for sync and subscriptions; enable Cloud AI only if you want cloud-assisted review.

Many features work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is required for cloud sync of Active Cases, Cloud AI, subscription entitlements, and Restore Purchases.

4.7 Price Compare (reference pricing)

Shows illustrative charge ranges from bundled sample data. Not live CMS hospital files, machine-readable hospital prices, or real-time insurer rates.

IssueWhat to try
No hospitals for my ZIP Try another ZIP or CPT code. Results depend on sample data and patient ZIP/state in your profile.
“High” or “extreme” labels Heuristic labels for learning — confirm charges with your provider and EOB before disputing.
Doesn’t match my bill Expected: reference ranges ≠ your negotiated rate. Use as a conversation starter, not proof.
