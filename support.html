Welcome to BillShield support. This page explains how to get help, answers common questions, and describes what you can expect from us as an individual developer. Need help? Email hello@familymedvault.com

Typical response time: We aim to reply within 2–3 business days (U.S. time). Complex issues may take longer. Support is provided on a best-effort basis; we do not guarantee a specific resolution time or outcome.

When contacting us, please include:

  • A brief description of the issue and steps to reproduce it
  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • BillShield app version (Settings → scroll to bottom)
  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Pro)
  • Whether you are signed in with Apple and your plan (Free, Pro, Appeal Pack)
  • Screenshots if helpful (redact member IDs, account numbers, and other sensitive details)

Privacy tip: Do not email full medical bills or EOBs unless absolutely necessary. If you must share document text, redact member IDs, dates of birth, and account numbers whenever possible.

2. Getting Started

  1. Scan or import a medical bill or EOB using the camera or a PDF.
  2. Review results on-device: flagged charges, denial-code explanations, and potential savings estimates.
  3. Save as an Active Case to track a billing dispute or appeal (subject to your plan limit).
  4. Track deadlines for internal appeals, external review, and state insurance complaints.
  5. Generate appeal letters and export PDFs when ready.
  6. Optional: Sign in with Apple to save history and sync Active Cases; enable Cloud AI only if you want cloud-assisted analysis (off by default).

Many core features work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is required for saving synced history, Active Case sync, Cloud AI, subscription linking, and Restore Purchases.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

4.1 Account & Sign in with Apple

IssueWhat to try
I can’t save bills, cases, or appeals Open Settings → tap Sign in with Apple. Saving, Active Cases, and sync require an authenticated session.
Sign in fails or loops Confirm you are signed into iCloud on the device; check network connection; force-quit and reopen the App; ensure iOS is up to date. If the problem persists, email us with your iOS version and app version.
I switched devices Sign in with the same Apple ID on the new device, then tap Restore Purchases. Local-only data from your old device is not transferred unless you had signed in and synced before.
How do I sign out? Settings → Sign out. Signing out disables Cloud AI and may limit synced features until you sign in again.
Delete my cloud account First use Settings → Privacy → Delete all data for on-device data. To request deletion of cloud account data (profiles, cases, usage logs), email hello@familymedvault.com with subject BillShield Account Deletion. See our Privacy Policy.

4.2 Active Cases & Plan Limits

IssueWhat to try
“Active Case limit reached” / can’t save a new case Your plan limits how many billing disputes you can track at once. Close, archive, or delete an existing Active Case, or upgrade to BillShield Pro in Settings. The Dashboard may show usage such as “1 / 1 used.”
Case count looks wrong after upgrade Sign in with Apple → Restore Purchases → wait a minute and reopen the App. If still incorrect, email us with your Apple ID subscription status (from Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions).
What is an Active Case? An Active Case is a saved billing dispute or appeal you are working on (linked bills/EOBs, denial codes, status). It is separate from a one-time scan that you do not save.

4.3 Cloud AI Assistance (Optional)

Cloud AI is disabled by default. When enabled, selected document text is sent through our secure backend (Supabase) to OpenAI for analysis. You never need to provide your own OpenAI API key.

IssueWhat to try
Cloud AI unavailable / “Not connected” Sign in with Apple first → Settings → enable Cloud AI Assistance. Status should show you are signed in and Cloud AI is ready.
Cloud AI enabled but scan uses local analysis only When scanning, check Include this document in Cloud AI Review for that document. Cloud AI is per-document opt-in even when the global toggle is on.
“Usage limit reached” / insufficient credits Cloud AI usage is subject to server-side limits tied to your plan. Allowances reset each billing cycle (not rolled over). BillShield Pro subscribers receive higher limits. You may see summarized usage in Settings.
Cloud AI slow or times out Check your internet connection. The App automatically falls back to on-device analysis if the cloud service is unavailable, slow, or returns an error—your scan should still complete locally.
I want to stop cloud processing Turn off Cloud AI Assistance in Settings and uncheck per-document Cloud AI options when scanning. See our Privacy Policy for data handling details.

4.4 Subscriptions, Pro & Appeal Pack

IssueWhat to try
Restore Pro on a new device Sign in with Apple first (same Apple ID used to purchase) → Settings → Restore Purchases.
Subscription not recognized after restore Confirm the purchase was made with the same Apple ID; wait a few minutes and try Restore again; check Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device. Contact Apple if the subscription does not appear in your Apple account.
Cancel or change plan Manage in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated by Apple.
Appeal Pack not applied Appeal Pack is a consumable one-time purchase. Sign in → complete purchase in Settings → tap Restore Purchases if benefits do not appear. Consumables are tied to your Apple ID and may be used when you run premium appeal features.

4.5 Scanning, OCR & Analysis

IssueWhat to try
Poor OCR / missing line items Use good lighting; hold the camera steady; avoid glare; try importing a PDF if available; use a higher-resolution photo. Multi-page bills may need separate scans.
Denial codes not recognized Many common CO-, PR-, OA-, PI-, and N- codes are supported on-device. Unusual or proprietary codes may show limited explanation—always verify with your insurer’s EOB legend.
Savings estimates seem wrong Estimates are educational and based on heuristics, benchmarks, and optional AI output. They are not guarantees. Verify all amounts with your provider and insurer before disputing charges.
App works offline On-device OCR, denial decoding, timelines, and local appeal templates work without internet. Cloud AI, sign-in sync, and subscription validation require connectivity.

4.6 Appeals, Letters & Reminders

IssueWhat to try
Appeal reminders not firing Settings → enable notifications → allow permission in iOS Settings → BillShield → Notifications. Do Not Disturb and Focus modes may suppress alerts.
Export PDF fails Ensure sufficient device storage; try again on Wi‑Fi; update iOS. If exporting to Files or Mail, confirm the destination app is available.
Premium appeal review May be included with BillShield Pro or added via a one-time Appeal Pack purchase. When available, it uses Cloud AI for deeper appeal analysis. Results are drafts only—you must review and submit appeals yourself.
Appeal letter content Letters are drafts for your review and editing. You are responsible for accuracy and submission to your insurer. The Developer does not file appeals on your behalf.
