3. Description of Service

BillShield helps U.S. consumers understand medical bills and insurance Explanation of Benefits (EOBs). Features may include:

  • Local-first on-device scanning/OCR, denial-code explanations, appeal timelines, negotiation scripts, and offline appeal templates;
  • Active Cases — saved billing disputes or appeals, subject to plan limits;
  • Optional Cloud AI Assistance (off by default; global toggle plus per-document opt-in when enabled);
  • Price Compare — illustrative charge ranges from bundled sample data (not live CMS or hospital price files);

The App is an educational and organizational tool only. It does not provide medical, legal, insurance, billing-advocacy, or financial advice. The Developer is not a healthcare provider, insurer, attorney, or licensed patient advocate and does not file appeals or negotiate bills on your behalf.
