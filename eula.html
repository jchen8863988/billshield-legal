3. Description of Service

BillShield helps U.S. consumers understand medical bills and insurance Explanation of Benefits (EOBs). The App is local-first by default. Features may include, without limitation:

  • Scanning or importing bills and EOBs (camera, photo library, PDF, pasted text) with on-device OCR and audit rules;
  • Plain-language explanations of denial/adjustment codes;
  • Comparison of charges to Medicare reference benchmarks and reference pricing estimates;
  • Common appeal timeline estimates and optional local reminders (not official insurer deadlines);
  • Editable appeal letter drafts and PDF export marked as drafts—you must review, edit, and submit appeals to your insurer yourself;
  • Educational hospital negotiation script templates (copy-to-clipboard);
  • Typical local charge ranges from illustrative bundled sample data in Price Compare (not live Hospital Price Transparency files or guaranteed market prices);
  • Local bill archive, family-member labels, and case timeline views;
  • Optional yearly out-of-pocket estimates and CSV export for your records (not professional tax preparation);
  • Reference links to financial assistance and medical billing advocacy resources (curated; not a comprehensive eligibility engine);
  • Active Cases — saved billing disputes or appeals, subject to plan limits;
  • Optional Cloud AI Assistance (disabled when you first use the App; requires your explicit enablement in Settings and per-document opt-in when scanning);
  • Optional paid features via Apple In-App Purchase, including BillShield Pro auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time Appeal Pack consumable.

The App is an educational and organizational tool only. It does not provide medical, legal, insurance, billing-advocacy, or tax advice. The Developer is not a healthcare provider, insurer, attorney, tax preparer, or licensed patient advocate. The Developer does not file appeals, contact insurers on your behalf, or negotiate bills for you.

8. Cloud AI (Optional — Off by Default)

Cloud AI is disabled when you first use the App. Unless you turn it on, bill and EOB content is processed only on your iPhone or iPad.

To use Cloud AI you must all of the following:

  1. Sign in with Apple and maintain a valid authenticated session;
  2. Turn on Cloud AI Assistance in Settings; and
  3. For each scan, separately enable include this document in Cloud AI review (per-document opt-in). The App does not enable per-document cloud review automatically even if the global toggle is on.

When enabled and opted in:

  • You provide explicit consent under our Privacy Policy;
  • Selected document text may be sent to our Supabase Edge Function (ai-proxy) and processed by a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI);
  • You do not supply your own AI provider API key;
  • Usage limits apply on our servers according to your plan;
  • If the cloud service fails, times out, or credits are exhausted, the App may fall back to on-device analysis or offline templates where possible.

You may disable Cloud AI at any time in Settings. Disabling stops new cloud uploads. See the Privacy Policy for data handling, retention, and deletion.
