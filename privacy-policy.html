2. What BillShield Does (and Does Not Do)

BillShield is an educational and organizational tool for U.S. consumers who want to understand medical bills, insurance Explanation of Benefits (EOBs), and related appeal steps. Features include, among others:

  • Scanning or importing bills and EOBs (camera, photo library, PDF, pasted text)
  • On-device audit rules, denial-code explanations, and Medicare benchmark comparisons
  • Common appeal timeline estimates (not official insurer deadlines)
  • Editable appeal letter drafts and PDF export—you submit appeals to your insurer yourself
  • Hospital negotiation script templates (educational)
  • Reference pricing estimates and typical local charge ranges (illustrative sample data—not live hospital price files)
  • Local bill archive, family-member labels, case timeline, and optional yearly out-of-pocket estimates (including optional CSV export for your records)
  • Reference links to financial assistance and medical billing advocacy resources (curated; not a comprehensive government database)

BillShield is not medical, legal, insurance, billing-advocacy, or tax advice. The Developer is not a healthcare provider, insurer, law firm, tax preparer, or licensed patient advocate.

The Developer is not a HIPAA “covered entity” or “business associate” unless separately agreed in writing (not offered by default). OCR, rule-based outputs, AI-assisted drafts, savings estimates, and timeline dates may be incomplete or wrong. You must verify all amounts, codes, deadlines, and procedures with your insurer, provider, and qualified professionals.

6. Local-First Processing & Optional Cloud AI

6.1 Default: on your device

Unless you enable Cloud AI, bill and EOB content is processed only on your iPhone or iPad. OCR (including Apple Vision where available), denial decoding, timeline math, Medicare benchmarks, reference price ranges, negotiation templates, and offline appeal letter templates do not upload document content to our servers.

6.2 Optional Cloud AI (off by default)

Cloud AI is disabled when you first use the App. To use it you must:

  1. Sign in with Apple and maintain a valid authenticated session with our backend;
  2. Turn on Cloud AI Assistance in Settings; and
  3. For each scan, separately check include this document in Cloud AI review (per-document opt-in).

When enabled and opted in:

  • Selected document text is sent from the App to our Supabase Edge Function (ai-proxy), which forwards it to OpenAI for processing.
  • Requests require your authenticated JWT; anonymous cloud access is blocked.
  • Usage is metered and limited on our servers according to your plan.
  • If the cloud service fails, times out, or credits are exhausted, the App may fall back to on-device analysis or offline templates where possible.

You can disable Cloud AI anytime in Settings. Disabling stops new cloud uploads; it does not by itself delete data already processed by third-party processors subject to their retention policies.

We do not use your name or branding of any AI model in the App interface as an endorsement. OpenAI is listed here only as a subprocessors for transparency.
