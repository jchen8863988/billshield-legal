5. Plans, Active Cases & AI Usage Limits

5.1 Active Cases (what you see in the App)

BillShield tracks open billing work as Active Cases (saved disputes/appeals). Your plan caps how many may be open at once. The App may display usage such as “1 / 1 used.” If you reach your limit, you must resolve or remove a case, or upgrade, before opening a new one.

5.2 Cloud AI credits (server-enforced)

When Cloud AI is enabled, usage is metered on our servers by AI credits tied to your plan. Credits are consumed by cloud actions (e.g., deep bill analysis, appeal letters). You may see summarized usage in Settings. Detailed logs support security and billing integrity.

Unused AI credits do not roll over to the next billing cycle unless we expressly state otherwise in the App or EULA.

6. Local-First Processing & Optional Cloud AI

6.1 Default: on-device

Unless you enable Cloud AI, bill and EOB content is processed only on your iPhone or iPad. OCR (Apple Vision), denial decoding, timelines, and offline appeal templates do not upload document content to our servers.

6.2 Optional Cloud AI (off by default)

If you enable Cloud AI Assistance in Settings (and, for scans, check per-document cloud review):

  • You must Sign in with Apple and hold a valid Supabase authentication session.
  • Selected document text is sent from the App to our Supabase Edge Function (ai-proxy), which forwards it to OpenAI for processing.
  • Requests require your authenticated JWT; anonymous cloud access is blocked.
  • Usage is metered and limited on our servers according to your plan.
  • If the cloud service fails, times out, or credits are exhausted, the App may fall back to on-device analysis where possible.

You can disable Cloud AI anytime in Settings. Disabling stops new cloud uploads; it does not by itself delete data already processed by third-party processors subject to their retention policies.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.
