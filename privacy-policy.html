4. How We Use Information

  • Provide core features: scan/OCR, denial-code explanations, appeal timelines, templates, and local audit rules.
  • Save your bills and appeals when you sign in with Apple.
  • Process optional Cloud AI requests you explicitly enable (deep analysis, appeal-letter drafts).
  • Manage subscriptions via Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat.
  • Send local push notifications for appeal deadlines (only if you enable them).
  • Improve security, prevent abuse, and enforce Cloud AI usage limits.
  • Respond to support requests you send to us.

5. Local-First & Cloud AI (Critical)

5.1 Default: on-device processing

Unless you turn on Cloud AI, bill and EOB content is processed only on your iPhone or iPad. OCR (Apple Vision), denial decoding, timelines, and offline appeal templates do not require uploading document content to our servers.

5.2 Optional Cloud AI

If you enable Cloud AI Assistance in Settings (and, for scans, check per-document cloud review):

  • You must Sign in with Apple and obtain a Supabase authentication session.
  • Selected document text (not raw image files by default in our extraction pipeline) is sent from the App to our Supabase Edge Function (ai-proxy), which forwards it to OpenAI for processing.
  • Requests include your authenticated user token; anonymous API access to Cloud AI is blocked (verify_jwt=true).
  • Monthly usage limits are enforced on our servers.

You can turn Cloud AI off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops new cloud uploads; it does not by itself delete data already processed by third-party processors subject to their retention policies.

Medical billing documents may contain Protected Health Information (PHI) or other sensitive personal data. We treat this data carefully:

  • Cloud AI opt-in: you direct us to transmit selected text to our backend and OpenAI for analysis.

If you are a HIPAA-covered entity or business associate, do not use BillShield to process PHI subject to a BAA unless you have a separate written agreement with the Developer (not offered by default).

9. Data Retention

  • On device: until you delete data in Settings → Privacy → Delete all data, or uninstall the App.
  • Supabase account data: retained while your account is active; you may request deletion (see Section 11).
  • Cloud AI usage logs: monthly counters retained for quota and billing integrity.
  • Support emails: retained as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, then deleted or anonymized.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures, including:

  • TLS encryption in transit for cloud features;
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) so authenticated users access only their own rows;
  • JWT-required Edge Functions for Cloud AI;
  • No storage of OpenAI API keys in the App binary or user settings (server-side secrets only);
  • On-device encrypted storage for sensitive preferences (Secure Store).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.
